Selection Box

This dedicated scene uses mixed content types as direct children of selection-box. Click to single-select, Shift+click to add more, or drag across empty space to marquee-select multiple items.

Stage size is fixed at 800x600 so the selection bounds stay predictable. The three selectable direct children below are an img, an inline svg, and a semantic article.

Combined Stage

This stage lets selection-box manage selection while drag-box and transform-box keep handling movement and transforms. Plain click picks one item, Shift+click adds more selections, and movement only starts after an item is already selected.

These buttons sit outside the stage and move or layer the currently selected items. Double-click a selected item to bring the selected group to front.
